The sidebar is `zr-field` green, full height with square edges, with `zr-mist` text, group labels in `zr-amber` and the active item marked by a 3px `zr-sage` bar on the start edge (left in English, right in Arabic) plus `aria-current="page"`. It lists only what the signed-in role may open; absent items are not greyed out.

The language toggle is a two-button pill, EN and عربي, always visible in the header of every screen including login, and it keeps the current screen and selection. It sets `lang` and `dir` on the document and stores the choice per user.

Tabs switch views of one object (a parcel's Overview, Evidence and History). The selected tab is marked by a `brand` underline and darker text, not colour alone. On phones the sidebar becomes a bottom bar of at most four items.
