A field photo is evidence only with its provenance beside it: timestamp with offset, GPS and accuracy, the agronomist id, the parcel id and a SHA-256 of the original file. Show them as text under the image in `data` style; the image is never shown without them.

State the relation to the declared boundary with a StatusPill: Inside boundary (confirmed) or Outside boundary (not-assessable, grey, because a photo does not by itself falsify a declaration). Photos are immutable after upload; show no edit, crop or filter control.

Photos never carry faces of people as a subject; blur any person before display and keep the original unblurred in the vault. Do not use stock photography in the product. Marketing surfaces may use the Zr3i wheat photography only under a `zr-navy` scrim.
