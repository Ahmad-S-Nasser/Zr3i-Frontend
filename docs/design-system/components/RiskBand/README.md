The risk band is a square chip with a 24px bold letter, a one-word label and the band colour (`band-a` to `band-e`, letter in `on-band-*`). A is lowest risk, E highest. The chip is never shown without the index name, the percentile and the maturity badge.

Bands belong to the risk module only. Do not use them for verdicts, status or decoration, and never show a band as a bare colour.

The maturity badge (`maturity-bg`, `maturity-ink`) states `Specified`, `Calibrated` or `Validated`, in upper-case Roboto. For the CRI and FRI method FRI-2026-01 it reads "Specified · not calibrated" and the method version is printed beside it. A tenant cannot hide it.

Copy: "production-risk percentile". Never score, rating, probability of default, approval or limit.
